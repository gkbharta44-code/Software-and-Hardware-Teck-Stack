Hybrid hardware stack

Four tiers from the device in the home up to the cloud. Work stays as close to the customer as the job allows, and authoritative records stay central. Specifications for home, factory edge, data centre and cloud are planning estimates, not vendor figures.

Sizing assumptions used in the cloud figures

  • 6.0 billion customers from Year 5 (from the summary).
  • 10 events per customer per day, combined across retail, bulk and TV. A placeholder to validate first.
  • About 200 bytes per event; peak at three times the daily average.
  • Result: about 694,000 events per second on average, about 2.1 million at peak, about 4.4 PB of raw event data per year.

Cloud platform

Proposed

Transaction backbone, lakehouse, identity and managed AI. Holds the authoritative cross-stream records.

Event backbone
Kafka or Confluent, roughly 10 to 30 brokers at typical throughput, to be benchmarked. Partitioned by region.
Lakehouse
Object storage on the order of petabytes per year at the assumed volume, with tiering to cold storage.
Compute
Kubernetes cluster sized for peak, with autoscaling.
AI
Managed LLM endpoints for the control plane. GPU capacity sized from actual workload, not estimated here.
Regions
Indian cloud regions for DPDP Act data residency. Multi-zone, with cross-region disaster recovery.
Security
Encryption at rest and in transit, HSM-backed keys, ISO 27001 controls, audit logging.

Dependencies: cloud provider not named in the summary beyond SAP BTP.

Open items before this stack is final

  • Events per customer per day, by stream. This drives the cloud and backbone sizing more than any other input.
  • Written sizing from SAP for S/4HANA and treasury, plus a transaction throughput model for retail, bulk and Live TV at peak.
  • Costs for the lines not in the budget: lakehouse, event backbone, cloud hosting, AI platform, identity, consolidation and treasury software.
  • Agreements not yet signed: ONDC, SGS, foundry contracts, and the Axis Bank escrow partnership. The plan should show what happens if any one falls through.
  • Regulatory approval for the Delper Token. Its structure affects settlement, the ledger design, and the tax treatment, so counsel should review it before design work.
  • Factory-edge hardware specifications, from the equipment vendors.
  • The forward-deployed engineer headcount. The summary's figures do not reconcile: seven per hub does not match 560,000 per year across 560,000 factories.
