Delper Integrated: software and hardware stack

Which systems the business process runs on, what each one does, and how far along it is. Components are drawn from the Executive Summary; nothing here is independently verified.

Commerce and business process

The systems that run the bulk trade track and the retail marketplace.

  • SAP BTP and Signavio Budgeted: $0.86M Process mapping middleware for the bulk trade core.
  • SAP S/4HANA core ERP Budgeted: $0.39M Core accounting and operations for the bulk track.
  • Shopify, WooCommerce and ONDC connectors Budgeted: $0.30M Buyer-side endpoints for the retail marketplace. ONDC is the proposed channel and has no agreement yet.
  • Verification ledger and threat ingestion Budgeted inside the $1.60M core team line Custom platform engineering that records verified transactions.

Trust, settlement and token

How money and quality checks move between buyers, sellers and the group.

  • Escrow with Axis Bank Partnership in progress Holds buyer payments. Bulk settles on a 14-day cycle; retail releases after delivery and a satisfaction certificate.
  • Third-party quality control: SGS No agreement yet Independent inspection, with webhook links from SGS results into the ERP.
  • Delper Token (ERC-20) Depends on regulatory approval The fiat-backed token the whole programme runs on, with a 2% tax on mint and burn.

Industrial security and control

Software that protects factory floor systems, and enforces the rules they run under.

  • Delper Aegis security layer Targets IEC 62443 Security Level 4 An inline firewall in front of each production line, with a physical write-lock against remote programming.
  • Industrial protocol baselining Part of the 60-day shadow mode Records Modbus and PROFINET traffic before any live enforcement begins.
  • Human override and audit log Policy required before go-live Every automated decision that affects customers, workers or safety needs a named owner, thresholds and a log of each use.

Hardware

Chips, network gear and the field equipment the software controls.

  • Delper Guard inline units 1,963,334 units at full scale Deployed across SME and enterprise tiers, with an 82.3% target margin on advanced chips and 68% on SME chips.
  • Fabrication: 28nm, 65nm and 5nm nodes Wafer agreements not yet signed Phase 1 uses 28nm and 65nm. Advanced dark-factory chips use 5nm FinFET. Multi-year foundry agreements are a validation gate.
  • Private 5G and anti-drone cover Part of the coastal defence plan Air-gapped private 5G cores at the factory sites, with anti-drone interceptor domes.
  • Village modules Part of the rural build-out Solar cold-storage nodes, telehealth rooms, water units and edge inverters for grid connection.

Operations and people

Who runs the systems once they are live.

  • Forward-deployed engineers Seven per dark-factory hub Run 24/7 monitoring across three 8-hour shifts. Trained through a 12-month online diploma with UVCE and Bangalore University.

What the summary does not yet specify

  • A cloud provider or hosting architecture beyond SAP BTP.
  • The AI model or agent platform behind customs decisions.
  • The database, data warehouse and identity layer.
  • Automation for the pilot, which uses manual bidding and human QC.
  • The engineer headcount: 560,000 FDEs per year does not match seven per hub across 560,000 factories.
